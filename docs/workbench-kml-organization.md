# KML tree organization

A KML Layer retains one document with ordered folders and Places. These composition
edits preserve Catalog identities, source provenance and access.

Each KML item has a **Move** handle. Drag above or below another item to change
its order, into the middle of a folder to move inside it, or onto the document's
drop area to move to its root. The move preserves Place identities, metadata,
geometry and folder contents. A closed destination stays closed and receives
focus on its visible heading. Select **Move** to use the Parent and up/down
controls with a keyboard or touch. Save the Project to retain the new position.
Moves across KML Layers, foreign drags, folder cycles and stale or unauthorized
controls are refused.

Local Project-file checks do not establish hosted account persistence or physical-device support.
